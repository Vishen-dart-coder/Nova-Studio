Questions before we begin

Can’t find your answer? Write to us and we’ll reply within two working days.

Are you taking on new projects?

Get in touch with a few details about what you’re building and we’ll let you know about availability and the best next step.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is scoped individually. After an initial conversation we send a clear proposal with scope, timeline and fixed investment before any work begins.

Do you work with early-stage brands?

Yes. We work with ambitious brands at different stages, from a first identity and website to a refresh of an established presence.

What do you need to get started?

A short brief is enough: what you’re building, who it’s for, and any references you love. We shape the rest together in discovery.

Can you design and build the website?

Yes. Design and development happen together, so the finished site matches the design precisely and performs well on every device.

Who owns the final work?

Ownership of final assets and files is agreed in the proposal so that expectations are clear from the start.

Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information this website handles. The short version: the site uses privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics to count visits, and does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It has no forms. The only things stored are two small settings in your own browser.

Who we are

Nøva is an independent digital design studio. You can reach us at iamvishensharma@gmail.com for anything in this policy.

What we collect

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they view. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It records anonymous page views, including the page address, the referring site, your country, and your browser, operating system and device type. We do not use it to identify individuals.

Apart from that, we do not collect personal information automatically. There are no accounts, sign-up forms, or contact forms on this site. If you choose to email us, we receive the email address, name and message you send. We use that only to reply to you and to discuss a possible project.

Cookies and local storage

The site does not set tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for two essential preferences. Neither is sent to us or to anyone else.

NamePurposeLasts
nova-modeRemembers whether you chose light or dark modeUntil you clear site data
nova-cookieRemembers that you have seen the cookie noticeUntil you clear site data

You can remove both at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings, or reopen the notice with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Third parties

Fonts and images are served from this site, so your browser does not contact font or image providers for them. Some collaboration cards link to pages on HorizonX (horizonx.so). If you follow those links, HorizonX’s own privacy policy applies. Vercel processes the analytics data described above under its own privacy policy. The company that hosts this website may also keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address and browser type, to run and secure the service.

How long we keep emails

We keep emails for as long as they are needed to handle your enquiry or project, and for any period the law requires. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing the address above. You can also use your browser to block or clear local storage; the site works without it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Cookies and storage

We don’t use tracking or advertising cookies. We count visits with cookie-free analytics, and this site only stores your light/dark choice and this notice in your browser.
